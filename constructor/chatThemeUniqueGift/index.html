chatThemeUniqueGift

Конструктор из схемы TL.

A chat theme based on a collectible gift ».

Определение TL

chatThemeUniqueGift#3458f9c8 gift:StarGift theme_settings:Vector<ThemeSettings> = ChatTheme;

Параметры

Имя Тип Описание
gift StarGift The owned or hosted collectible gift » on which this theme is based, as a starGiftUnique constructor.
theme_settings Vector<ThemeSettings> Theme settings.

Тип

ChatTheme

Related pages

Telegram Gifts

Users can send Gifts to their friends. The recipients of gifts can display them on their profile pages or turn them into Telegram Stars ». Telegram Stars can be used for many things, including supporting creators and buying services in mini apps.

starGiftUnique

Represents a collectible star gift, see here » for more info.

The sticker that represents the gift is contained in a starGiftAttributeModel object in attributes.
