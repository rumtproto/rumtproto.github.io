Blackberry 10 Dev Contest

« Back to Contest Announcement

To give an incentive to Telegram fans among Blackberry engineers, we are announcing a month-long coding contest ending on December 13, 2014. The task is to port Telegram to BB 10 using our open source Android code.

Conditions

  1. Your app must have it's own api_id.
  2. Your code must be released under an open source license.
  3. Please don't publish your code before we announce the winners.
  4. Please don't use the name Telegram for your apps.
  5. Please don't use the official Telegram logos.
