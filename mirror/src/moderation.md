# Telegram Safety Overview

## Total Groups and Channels Blocked

Telegram blocks **tens of thousands** of groups and channels daily and removes **millions** of pieces of content that violate its [Terms of Service](/tos/), including incitement to violence, sharing child abuse materials, and trading illegal goods.  
  
Since 2015, moderation has combined **user reports** with **proactive monitoring** powered by machine learning. In early 2024, this effort was further enhanced with cutting-edge **AI moderation tools**.

## CSAM Groups and Channels Banned

Telegram enforces a zero-tolerance policy for Child Sexual Abuse Materials (CSAM). Since 2018, public images are automatically checked against a **hash database** of CSAM banned by our moderators over the past decade.  
  
In 2024, we expanded this database with hashes from organizations like the [Internet Watch Foundation](https://www.iwf.org.uk/news-media/news/telegram-joins-iwf-in-child-sexual-abuse-imagery-crackdown/). Telegram publishes [daily transparency reports](https://t.me/stopCA) on the removal of CSAM content.

## Banned Terrorist Communities

Calls to violence and terrorist propaganda have no place on Telegram. Since 2016, Telegram has published [daily transparency reports](https://t.me/ISISwatch) on the removal of terrorist content. Telegram’s anti-terrorism efforts have been [recognized by Europol](https://www.europol.europa.eu/media-press/newsroom/news/referral-action-day-six-eu-member-states-and-telegram).  
  
Since 2022, Telegram has significantly scaled its efforts in partnership with organizations like ETIDAL, the Global Center for Combating Extremist Ideology. Through its collaboration with ETIDAL alone, Telegram’s moderators have removed [over 200 million](https://etidal.org/en/etidal-and-telegram-are-combating-digital-extremism-by-removing-97-million-content-during-2025/) pieces of terrorist content.
