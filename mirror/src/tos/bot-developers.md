### 7\. Blockchain Integration

To maintain a highly scalable, smooth and secure user experience in line with Telegram's standards, all **Mini Apps** which implement cryptocurrency functionality, either within the Mini App itself or within its connected bot, are required to be based exclusively on [The Open Network (TON)](https://ton.org/) blockchain.

> For specific examples of services that are permitted or not permitted by these terms, reference our [blockchain guidelines](/bots/blockchain-guidelines/).

#### 7.1. Issuing Assets

If your Mini App integrates functionality related to the generation or distribution of cryptocurrency tokens or blockchain assets, it must exclusively use [The Open Network (TON)](https://ton.org/) blockchain for the creation and distribution of said tokens or assets.

#### 7.2. Connecting Wallets

Any Mini App that utilizes functionality related to cryptocurrency wallets must only interface with wallets connected via the [TON Connect SDK](https://docs.ton.org/v3/guidelines/ton-connect/overview). This includes, but is not limited to, user authorization and signing of blockchain transactions, as well as the sending and receipt of any cryptocurrency tokens or blockchain assets.

For clarity, this applies both to Mini Apps that directly integrate cryptocurrency wallets and those that indirectly interact with cryptocurrency wallets in any capacity. Using other wallet connection protocols is permitted for the purpose of enabling users to bridge assets from other blockchains.

#### 7.3. Multichain Wallets

Mini Apps that function as multichain wallets, whether they be custodial or non-custodial, are permitted to let users manage assets on other blockchains, provided that such actions are performed directly within the interface of the Mini App. If available, any interactions with other apps and platforms must exclusively be carried out via [TON Connect](https://docs.ton.org/v3/guidelines/ton-connect/overview).
