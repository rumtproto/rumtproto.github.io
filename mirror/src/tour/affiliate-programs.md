# Affiliate Programs

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400631/3/T9GeGE_82t0.50255.png/1a1174fbf6b364b5ae">

Developers are able to open affiliate programs for their mini app – allowing **content creators**, other **mini app developers** and **any Telegram user** to promote it and **earn commissions** on purchases made by people they referred.

#### Joining Affiliate Programs

Telegram users are able to browse a list of **active affiliate programs** from their **Star balance** in _Settings > My Stars_. There they can sort programs by their start date, total revenue or potential profit and join **any program** they wish. There are **no limits** on the number of programs a user can join.

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400665/4/GkcA073_qLM.333156/ef09de2b30fd53fd14" title="List of programs" width="480" class="dev_page_image">](https://telegram.org/file/400780400665/4/GkcA073_qLM.333156/ef09de2b30fd53fd14)

Joining a program generates a **unique link** for you to use. When creating the link, you **choose a Star balance** to connect to your link – the balance of your **personal account**, or alternatively, the balance of a **channel** you own or **mini app** you develop.

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400271/3/Z4eTgGgIK4c.286284/1bd9b3af02371c6198" title="Choose balance" width="480" class="dev_page_image">](https://telegram.org/file/400780400271/3/Z4eTgGgIK4c.286284/1bd9b3af02371c6198)

This determines where the Stars from your commissions **will be sent** and how they **can be spent**. Make sure to consider carefully before choosing, as Stars **can't be transferred** between balances. For example, Stars sent to your personal balance can be used to **purchase products** or **subscriptions** in mini apps – but they can't be used to **create giveaways** in your channel or **increase limits** for your mini app.

> You can create **additional links** for the same program to earn Stars on [multiple balances](#q-can-i-change-which-star-balance-is-connected-to-my-referral-link) – such as for your channel or mini app, in addition to your personal account.

#### Sharing Your Referral Link

Once you have a referral link, you can share it anywhere – both inside and outside of Telegram. When another user starts a mini app **for the first time** via your referral link, all purchases they make there with [Telegram Stars](/blog/telegram-stars/) will **generate a commission** for you.

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400008/3/YS7ipZQan_c.288121/a9146239a15049cc0c" title="Receipt" width="480" class="dev_page_image">](https://telegram.org/file/400780400008/3/YS7ipZQan_c.288121/a9146239a15049cc0c)

Content creators and mini app developers can use their platforms to promote their link by including it in **channel posts** or **mini app interfaces**. You can also post your link on **social media sites**, add it to **your story**, share it in groups of friends or even convert it into a scannable QR code.

> Make sure to **share your link responsibly** – sending your referral link to strangers or posting it in public groups could lead to [temporary limitations](/faq/#can-t-send-messages-to-non-contacts) on your account.

#### Receiving Commissions

Any Telegram users who start the mini app **for the first time** after following a referral link will be considered a **successful referral** – and any purchases they make with [Telegram Stars](/blog/telegram-stars/) will **earn commissions** for the respective affiliate.

You will be able to receive commissions for the length of the **commission period** set by the program creator. For example, if a user you referred launches the relevant mini app on January 1 under a commission period of **30 days**, you will receive commissions from the purchases of that user **until January 31**.

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400705/1/Ig9Rpf4B3DA.310155/4ecd3ca2d6d5f61361" title="Receiving Commissions" width="480" class="dev_page_image">](https://telegram.org/file/400780400705/1/Ig9Rpf4B3DA.310155/4ecd3ca2d6d5f61361)

Stars from commissions go directly to the affiliate's **Star balance** – which can then be used to purchase [digital products](/blog/telegram-stars/#digital-goods-and-services) and [subscriptions](/blog/fullscreen-miniapps-and-more/#subscription-plans), unlock [paid content](/blog/mini-app-bar-paid-media-and-more/#pay-for-content-with-telegram-stars) in channels, [send gifts](/blog/gifts-verification-platform/#gifts) to contacts and [much more](#using-telegram-stars).

#### Creating Affiliate Programs

Developers can start an affiliate program from the **profile page** of their mini app. When creating the program, the developer sets a **commission rate** and **commission period** for the campaign. The rate dictates **what percentage** an affiliate will receive from qualifying purchases. The period determines **for how long** an affiliate will receive commissions from qualifying purchases.

Once a program has been created, its commission rate and period can **only be increased** from their initial values. If you wish to decrease these values, you will have to [terminate](#q-how-do-i-stop-my-mini-app-s-affiliate-program) the program and create a new one. Only the Telegram account that **owns the mini app** is able to create, modify or terminate its affiliate program.

> Affiliate programs are intended for mini apps that offer **digital products** for [Telegram Stars](/blog/telegram-stars/). Physical products sold via third-party payment providers do **not** give commissions for affiliates – see the [Terms of Service](/tos/) for more details.

#### FAQ:

##### Q: Can I change which Star balance is connected to my referral link?

No – after [creating your referral link](#joining-affiliate-programs), it is **not possible** to change which balance receives the commissions from people you refer via that link. To do so, you will need to **get a new link** and select your preferred balance for it.

You can do so at any time via _Settings > My Stars > Earn Stars > My Programs_ – tap a program to show your link, then **tap the profile** under _'Commissions will be sent to'_ to select the balance you want the new link to use. Note that doing so will issue a **new link** with a **different URL**.

Your previous link will **still work** – and you can quickly access it by switching back to that profile under _'Commissions will be sent to'_. As such, it's possible to utilize **multiple referral links** for one program – allowing any of your bots and channels to receive Stars, in addition to your personal account.

##### Q: How do I stop my mini app's affiliate program?

Once created, affiliate programs remain active until **manually terminated** by the program creator via the _Mini App Profile > Edit > Affiliate Program > End Affiliate Program_. Around 24 hours after manual termination, all active referral links tied to the program will be revoked.

However, affiliates will retain **all commissions** they earned before the program was terminated. Additionally, **future purchases** by users who were referred by affiliates that joined while the program was active will **continue generating commissions** for those affiliates until their originally specified **commission period** has elapsed.

For example, if an affiliate program had a commission period of 30 days, and a user you referred launched the relevant mini app on January 1, you would receive commissions from their purchases until January 31. If another user joined on January 14, you will receive commissions until February 13. If the creator terminates the program on January 15, no new affiliates will be able to join, but the existing affiliates who joined before termination will continue to receive commissions until the end of the respective commission periods.

##### Q: Can I leave an affiliate program?

Yes – if you wish to no longer participate as an affiliate, you can leave a program via _Settings > My Stars > Earn Stars_. Simply press and hold the program in your list and tap _'Leave'_ – this will revoke your current link. If you change your mind later, you can always rejoin the program if it's still active.
