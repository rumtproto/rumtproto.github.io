# Telegram Widgets

Telegram widgets offer a quick way to add a [**sharing button**](/widgets/share/) to your pages, [**embed posts**](/widgets/posts/) from public [channels](/tour/channels/) or [groups](/tour/groups/). Widgets can also help your users [**log in with Telegram**](/bots/telegram-login/) or view [**Telegram Discussions**](/widgets/discussion/) on your website.
