# Infinite Reactions, Emoji Statuses and Much More

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/464001934/109a2/2ll4Vk5UE-A.33543.png/0d0c693191833d0f46">

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/464001851/10bd6/sDwj4p_0m-0.293264/66eebb00fd46acb1ce" srcset="/file/464001958/116f8/uxZdNYY2XGc.640912/e01620ed9409be5ffc, 1200w" title="" alt="Infinite Reactions, Emoji Statuses and Much More">](https://telegram.org/file/464001958/116f8/uxZdNYY2XGc.640912/e01620ed9409be5ffc)

Telegram's [previous update](/blog/custom-emoji/) revolutionized emoji, adding an open platform for creating **custom animated emoji**. This update gives you even more ways to use these new emoji to express how you're feeling – with **infinite reactions** and **emoji statuses**.

#### Infinite Reactions

All users now have access to dozens of reactions – including those that were previously only available with Telegram Premium, like <img class="emoji" src="https://telegram.org/img/emoji/40/F09FA4A3.png" width="20" height="20" alt="🤣"> <img class="emoji" src="https://telegram.org/img/emoji/40/F09F9890.png" width="20" height="20" alt="😐"> <img class="emoji" src="https://telegram.org/img/emoji/40/F09F90B3.png" width="20" height="20" alt="🐳">. To accommodate all the new emoji, we've redesigned the **reaction panel**, making it **expandable**. The reactions you use frequently will show up at the top.

<img class="icon" src="https://telegram.org/file/464001305/110b0/-bpC5P3HJp4.6317/a2b79589744c739331" alt="Premium"> [**Telegram Premium**](/blog/700-million-and-premium/) users can pick reactions from an **infinite selection** of [custom emoji](/blog/custom-emoji/). To make choosing from **thousands** upon thousands of emoji easier, they can now attach up to **3 reactions per message**.

These changes to reactions are currently available in groups and 1-on-1 chats.

> **Group admins** can control whether custom reactions may be used in their groups.

#### <img class="icon" src="https://telegram.org/file/464001305/110b0/-bpC5P3HJp4.6317/a2b79589744c739331" alt="Premium"> Emoji Statuses

Starting today, Premium users can add an **animated emoji status** that is displayed **next to their name** – to quickly let everyone know how they're feeling or what they're doing. This custom status takes the place of your <img class="icon" src="https://telegram.org/file/464001986/11e90/OcmtMKhW_l8.1793/8c1c37d18dc5a0cabe" alt="Premium"> [Premium Badge](/blog/700-million-and-premium/#premium-badges) in the chat list, in your profile and in groups.

You can set one of 7 standard statuses that **change their color** to match different Telegram themes – or choose from an **infinite number** of [custom emoji](/blog/custom-emoji/#custom-animated-emoji). Popular suggestions for working, sleeping, traveling and more will be shown at the top.

Tap the Premium badge at the top of your chat list or go to Settings to **change your status**. Press and hold an emoji to set a status for a **specific duration**.

> Anyone can use Telegram's **open emoji platform** to [upload custom packs](/stickers/#custom-emoji) with unique art styles and characters for Telegram Premium users.

#### Improved Login Flow

Users who log out and log back in frequently can now receive login codes through their **email address** or using **Sign in with Apple** or **Sign in with Google**.

Signing up or logging in to Telegram on **iOS** is now even more streamlined, with new interfaces and fun animations that are already familiar to our Android users.

#### New Username Links

All **@usernames** on Telegram have their own _t.me/username_ links, making it easy to share your **public profile**, **group** or **channel** anywhere on the internet.

Every username now has a new unique link format – **username.t.me** – for those who want to emphasize their name (or have trouble finding the / key).

#### Prioritize Downloads on Android

Media and files that are [actively downloading](/blog/downloads-attachments-streaming/#download-manager) can be managed in the 'Downloads' tab of Search or by tapping the <img class="icon" src="https://telegram.org/file/464001063/11727/SID0JcEpgPY.968/db5945dc6265306c0f" alt="Downloads Android"> icon that appears in your chat list. You can now press and hold any items in the list to **reorder** them and change their **priority** – downloading the top file first.

New **smooth animations** have been added for **opening**, **closing** and **changing media** on Android.
