# Member Tags, Login with Telegram, Disable Sharing, GIF Editing, Date Formatting, Voting Timestamps

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400366/4/p5sPiCkIR8M.16212.png/c2c151574bf5030318">

In today's update, we add **tags** to help group members show who they are, a setting to **disable sharing messages** in specific 1-on-1 chats, **GIF editing and captions**, new tools for **signing up** and **logging in** to apps and websites with Telegram — and more.

#### Member Tags

Members in group chats can now **add a tag** next to their name — to **show their role** at work, **what they study** in school, and more. This helps you **introduce yourself** in neighborhood chats or **highlight your interests** in online communities.

Admins can choose whether group members are able to **set their own tags** or if **only admins** can assign them. Member tags of group admins are always displayed in a **different color** to make their roles clear.

> To allow members to set their tags in your group, go to _Group Settings > Permissions_ and turn on _'Edit Own Tags'_.

#### <img class="icon" src="https://telegram.org/file/464001305/110b0/-bpC5P3HJp4.6317/a2b79589744c739331" alt="Premium:"> Disable Sharing in Private Chats

In 2021, Telegram added [content protection settings](/blog/protected-content-delete-by-date-and-more/#protected-content-in-groups-and-channels) for **groups and channels**, which help communities and content creators make their posts **more exclusive**.

With today's update, users can now disable sharing **in any 1-on-1 chat** to foster an atmosphere of mutual trust. This **restricts message forwarding** and **prevents screenshots** or **saving media** from the chat.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400359/4/1GnghQ2Ng58.72446.jpg/12704e9101375c174e" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Disable Sharing in Private Chats"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400262/2/JsTVdkg80_s.7083436.mp4/49b41cf88543ff98a5" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

> To disable sharing in a chat, open a user's profile, tap ⋯ or ⋮ and select _'Disable Sharing'_. This feature is currently available to users of [Telegram Premium](https://t.me/premium).

#### Captions for GIFs

When selecting a GIF in the GIF panel, you can now **add a caption** directly to it — and send it all as **one message**. Just like with [other media](/blog/message-effects-and-more/#captions-above-media), your caption can be placed **above** or **below the GIF** for perfectly formatted memes.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400354/4/_aapIRnXJSY.62265.jpg/f1fb41b65e0d1a5a8e" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="GIF Captions"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400992/6/dVF5GyuaGQ4.6907460.mp4/9c070628f38ea21873" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

> To add a caption, press and hold in the GIF panel and select _'Add Caption'_.

#### Instant Stickers from Photos

Photos you open in the media viewer can now be **converted to stickers** in just **one tap**. These stickers [can be edited](/blog/sticker-maker/) to **cut out objects**, **add text** and **emoji** and more before sending.

<div class="blog_image_wrap blog_wide_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/400780400954/7/VJb3o2nsQ8Q.667262.jpg/6212ac211a87f2b399" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400954/7/VJb3o2nsQ8Q.667262.jpg/6212ac211a87f2b399" alt="Creating Stickers" srcset="/file/400780400954/7/VJb3o2nsQ8Q.667262.jpg/6212ac211a87f2b399 , 2x"></a></div>

> Stickers you create can be **saved to your favorites** or added to sticker packs.

#### Editing GIFs and Stickers

**GIFs and stickers** from the panel can now be edited in Telegram's [powerful media editor](/blog/hidden-media-zero-storage-profile-pics/#new-drawing-and-text-tools) — to **add text, drawings and emoji**, or to trim GIFs and **change their length**.

<div class="blog_image_wrap blog_wide_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/400780400372/4/gRsa4_5qsI8.442994.jpg/b03237c389d760dbcd" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400372/4/gRsa4_5qsI8.442994.jpg/b03237c389d760dbcd" alt="Editing Stickers" srcset="/file/400780400372/4/gRsa4_5qsI8.442994.jpg/b03237c389d760dbcd , 2x"></a></div>

> To edit a GIF or sticker in the panel, press and hold and select _'Edit'_.

#### Timestamps for Polls

Polls with visible votes now **show a timestamp** for each user who voted, so participants can see when each vote was cast.

#### Streaming Responses for Bots

Last year we gave [AI bots](/blog/comments-in-video-chats-threads-for-bots/#threads-and-streaming-responses-for-ai-bots) the ability to **stream text** as they generated a response. With today's update, **every bot** on Telegram can enable this feature — for **satisfying replies** that scroll across the screen.

#### Log In With Telegram

It’s now possible to use your Telegram account to **sign up** and **log in to apps and websites** in just a few taps — without filling out forms or creating passwords.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400374/5/0BP2y09Wxws.49592.jpg/74278cda319d681042" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Telegram Login"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400232/4/wpUPbbooUJY.7426390.mp4/8a8e497d6868d0a9ff" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

You can **share your phone number** to avoid extra verification steps. You can also let services **message you on Telegram** for convenient notifications.

> Developers can find more information about how to integrate Log In With Telegram in this [detailed guide](/bots/telegram-login/).

#### Time and Date Formatting

You can use the new **'Date'** formatting option to send a **specific time and date** in a message. Recipients can tap it to **add an event** to their calendar or set a **quick reminder**.

Date formatting **automatically adapts** to each recipient's **local date and time** — so no one has to do time-zone math.

Messages from bots can also utilize **dynamic time variables** to give users a precise date and time in a message, tailored to their time zone.
