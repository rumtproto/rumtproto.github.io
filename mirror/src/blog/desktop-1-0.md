### What Else Makes Telegram Desktop Cool?

The best thing about Telegram Desktop is that it automatically syncs messages from your phone with your computer using Telegram’s encrypted cloud. Just [install it](https://desktop.telegram.org) and log in to your account! All your messages, documents, photos and videos will be synced for you from the secure cloud.

Thanks to [Cloud Drafts](/blog/drafts/), you can even start typing on your phone, then continue on your desktop when you reach home or work.

Who needs email now? Telegram Desktop is many times faster and handles attachments like a pro. Just share your [t.me/username](https://t.me/username) with those who want to reach you.
