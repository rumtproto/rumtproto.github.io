# Shareable Chat Folders, Custom Wallpapers and More

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/464001391/12a08/mxuFQFqs9ig.226529/b33244c17e8def5591" srcset="/file/464001162/11835/u1_6h4Hig8o.406372/b4d17253d72e03769c" title="The hills are alive with the links of sharing." alt="Introducing Shareable Chat Folders, Custom Wallpapers and More">](https://telegram.org/file/464001162/11835/u1_6h4Hig8o.406372/b4d17253d72e03769c)

This update lets users **share entire chat folders** with one link, create **custom wallpapers** for individual chats, use **web apps** in any chat and more.

#### Shareable Chat Folders

[Chat folders](/blog/folders/) can now be **shared with a link** – inviting your friends or colleagues to dozens of work groups, collections of news channels, and more. With one tap, they will **add the folder** and **instantly join** all its chats.

Each chat folder supports **multiple invite links** allowing access to **different chats**. When creating a link, select the chats you'd like to include – and give it a **unique name** to match, like _Interns_ or _Managers_.

> You can include **any public chats**, as well as any chats where you have the admin rights to **add people**.

If you **add more chats** to your folder and **update its link**, members will see a suggestion to join the new chats.

> To **create a folder**, open _Settings > Chat Folders_. For more details, see our [in-depth guide](/tour/chat-folders/) on Shareable Folders.

#### Custom Wallpapers

Your **favorite photos** and **color combinations** can become custom wallpapers in specific chats to give conversations extra personality and make them stand out.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/464001402/11aae/nl3N1N49HEE.44483/6a3a84b1334da731d8" style="max-width: 600px;" title="Paper, paper on the wall, who's the background of them all?" alt="Custom Wallpapers"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/464001832/f630/9lxpbQNnZXc.3920159.mp4/34edf9fecb050276a4" type="video/mp4"></video></div>

After setting a wallpaper, a **special message** will be sent in the chat that allows your chat partner to add the **same wallpaper** on their side – or they can create their own.

> You can set a custom wallpaper in any 1-on-1 chat. To set a custom wallpaper, tap <img class="icon" src="https://telegram.org/file/464001466/3/TUYKwGzdaIM.984/c472ac8f01ebdd57c8" alt="⋮"> _\> 'Set Wallpaper'_ in the chat header on Android, or open a profile and tap <img class="icon" src="https://telegram.org/file/464001448/2/VHd-rEO8AMI.2898/1d9c403b5d0d54dbb0" alt="⋯"> _\> 'Change Wallpaper'_ on iOS.

#### Better Bots

Telegram bots are able to host [seamless web apps](/bots/webapps/), bringing **services** and **utilities** to millions of users – these **web apps** can now be launched in [any chat](/bots/webapps/#recent-changes).

Web apps of bots that support this feature can be accessed via a **direct link** or by **mentioning** the bot's username in any chat on Telegram. For example, try typing _@durgerkingbot_ (followed by a space) in any chat — or posting this link: [](https://t.me/DurgerKingBot/menu)[https://t.me/DurgerKingBot/menu](https://t.me/DurgerKingBot/menu)

When **launched in a group**, web apps can now support **collaboration** and **multiplayer** features for members.

This further expands the already vast opportunities for third-party developers and merchants on Telegram — free of charge for everyone. [Check out the API](/bots/) and start building!

> Subscribe to [@BotNews](https://t.me/botnews) for the latest updates about Telegram bots.

#### Bot Links and Telegram Premium on Fragment

Bots are now able to use [collectible usernames](/blog/topics-in-groups-collectible-usernames/#collectible-usernames) — including links **without** the _\-bot_ suffix.

You can also buy Telegram Premium for yourself or gift it to others via [Fragment](https://fragment.com/premium).

#### Fast Scrolling for Attachments

The attachment menu now scrolls at **super-fast speeds**, just like [Shared Media](/blog/shared-media-scrolling-calendar-join-requests-and-more/#hyper-speed-scrolling-for-shared-media). Pull down on the **date bar** to time travel.

#### Read Time in Topics

In groups of under **100 members** with [topics enabled](/blog/topics-in-groups-collectible-usernames/#topics-in-groups), read receipts now show [what time](/blog/power-saving/#read-time-in-small-groups) your messages were read by other group members.

#### Improved Interfaces

[Send When Online](/blog/verifiable-apps-and-more/#send-when-online) requires fewer taps – in chats where you can see your partner's last seen time, hold the Send button.

You can create groups **without adding members** immediately – in case you want to set up permissions and pin a few messages first.

On the latest iPhones, profile pictures have a **new animation** – smoothly sliding into the dynamic island when you scroll on **profiles** and **info pages**.
