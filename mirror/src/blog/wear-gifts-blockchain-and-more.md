# Wear Collectible Gifts, Move Gifts to the Blockchain, Send Gifts to Channels, and More

Today's update adds **6 new features** to Telegram, including the ability to **wear collectible gifts** as an emoji status, **move gifts** to the blockchain, **send gifts** to channels — and more.

#### Wear Your Unique Gifts

Gifts that you [upgrade to collectibles](/blog/collectible-gifts-and-more/#collectible-gifts) can now be used as a **unique emoji status**.

Using a gift as your status gives it a **glittering star effect** and **changes the appearance** of your profile to **match the backdrop** and symbol from your collectible.

> To wear one of your gifts, go to _My Profile > Gifts_, tap a gift and select _'Wear'_. If you take off the gift later, your profile will return to its original appearance.

#### Move Gifts to the Blockchain

Like [collectible usernames](/blog/topics-in-groups-collectible-usernames/#collectible-usernames) and [numbers](/blog/ultimate-privacy-topics-2-0/#sign-up-without-a-sim-card), gifts can now be **transferred** or **auctioned** via the TON blockchain. This gives owners **permanent control** — even if they lose access to or delete their Telegram account.

Moving a gift to a [TON wallet](https://ton.org/en/wallets?locale=en&pagination[limit]=-1) also allows you to connect to **outside services**, such as auction sites or platforms that offer **extra perks** for collectibles.

> To move gifts to the blockchain, go to _My Profile > Gifts_ and tap one of your gifts, then select _Transfer > Send via Blockchain_.

#### Give Gifts to Channels

To celebrate holidays and achievements, Telegram users can now **congratulate channels** by **sending gifts** that are displayed on the **channel's profile**.

Just like when sending gifts to friends, subscribers can use Stars to **upgrade the gift** before sending — letting the recipient convert it into a **unique collectible** for free.

> To send a gift to a channel, tap the <img class="emoji" src="https://telegram.org/img/emoji/40/F09F8E81.png" width="20" height="20" alt="🎁"> gift icon in the bottom corner of the channel.

Channel owners can **transfer their gifts** to other users and channels or **auction them** on the blockchain. Content creators and charities can use this functionality to **raise funds** for special projects and important causes.

> **Note:** Gifts to channels will be rolling out **gradually** and may not be available everywhere immediately. But everyone can already check out how this feature works in [this channel](https://t.me/durov) <img class="emoji" src="https://telegram.org/img/emoji/40/F09F9889.png" width="20" height="20" alt="😉">

#### Share Gifts

Collectible gifts on your profile can be **posted to your story** — generating an elegant **animated preview** to show off your rare artwork and lucky numbers.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400769/4/K4lgkiw7ZTg.38199/8bb91e5a9125d8ca6d" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Share to Story"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400191/4/QJCZmtRIuXQ.1537298.mp4/fe4e51597ac0f14cbc" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

> To post a gift to your story, go to _My Profile > Gifts_, tap one of your gifts and select _Share > Post to Story_.

Every collectible gift has a **unique link** in the format of `t.me/nft` which you can share with friends or post to a channel.

#### Filter Gifts on Channel Profiles

The _'Gifts'_ tab on the profiles of channels includes **filters** to sort gifts by their **value** and **collectible status**, the **date** they were received, and whether they are from a **limited** or **unlimited collection**.

Admins can **enable notifications** from the Gifts tab of their channels to keep track of the gifts that are sent to the channel.
