# My Profile, Recommended Channels and 15 More Features

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400694/4/d9kWf7j6sAQ.34227.png/732463eafcdd7dbf5e">

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400888/2/MqyrsvJhjQU.123192.webp/6fcd77ee03ce550034" srcset="/file/400780400847/5/aroFleyfoio.357050.webp/b747ca24023d373758, 1200w" title="" alt="Introducing My Profile, Recommended Channels and 15 More Features">](https://telegram.org/file/400780400847/5/aroFleyfoio.357050.webp/b747ca24023d373758)

This month we've added more than **15** new features, including **recommended channels**, a way to **view your own profile** and display **your channel** and **birthday** on your page, improved **moderation tools** for group admins – and much more.

#### Recommended Channels

The search interface has a new **_'Channels'_ tab** that shows the channels you follow. The tab also has a list of **recommended channels** with **similar topics** where you can discover new content creators.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400819/1/3eOT4tCn0O8.131658/e2fa203e81b4e9917b" style="max-width: 600px;" title="" alt="Recommended Channels"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400379/3/VDGFWrQqLwI.6525824.mp4/4c5dee5d41765f752b" type="video/mp4"></video></div>

When viewing posts from a channel, you can **pull up** to jump to the [next unread channel](/blog/live-streams-forwarding-next-channel/#jump-to-next-channel) in your list. After hopping through all your channels, you can keep scrolling to **browse recommended channels**.

> Channel recommendations are based only on the **general topic** of channels you already subscribe to. So any predominance of particular types of vegetables or fruit is entirely on you.

#### My Profile

Your Settings now have a **_'My Profile'_** section that allows you to view your profile as it appears to **other users** – and quickly edit or change any information.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400602/5/fkhCDMS7CD0.35064/812618382177196073" style="max-width: 600px;" title="" alt="My Profile"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400307/2/rqOOF4Zo130.2018761.mp4/7e924cc8d5eae8ef05" type="video/mp4"></video></div>

You can now **pin** up to **3 stories** to the top of your profile page – to showcase your achievements or make sure nobody misses that cat in a suit you met in an elevator.

> We've also added little icons that show the **privacy settings** of your posted stories, so you can quickly see if they need adjustments.

#### Birthdays

Anyone can choose to **add their birthday** to their profile – letting all their contacts know when to bring gifts. On your birthday, anyone who **visits your profile** will be greeted with animated confetti and plenty of balloons.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400799/7/yXhd_Y108KY.39614/024a22190a3b08dae8" style="max-width: 600px;" title="" alt="Birthdays"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400313/4/kw7t3ZYs8Fw.5014153.mp4/5f203b6889710fe75e" type="video/mp4"></video></div>

When it's someone else's birthday, a **banner** appears in your chat list – so you won't forget to congratulate them. Don't worry if you forgot their gift, you can **tap the banner** to quickly send a subscription to Telegram Premium.

> By default, your date of birth is only visible to **your contacts**. Like other information on your profile, birthdays have **granular privacy settings** which you can control via _Settings > Privacy and Security > Date of Birth_.

#### Channels on Your Profile

If you own a channel, you can now **add it to your profile page**. This places a **detailed preview** of the latest post above your bio – sending visitors to your personal project, online portfolio or company blog in **one tap**.

#### Collectibles on Your Profile

Owners of [collectible usernames](/blog/topics-in-groups-collectible-usernames/#collectible-usernames) can **link them** to their Telegram account or channels – listing the extra names **on the profile page**.

Tapping a collectible username or [anonymous number](/blog/ultimate-privacy-topics-2-0/#sign-up-without-a-sim-card) on someone's profile now pulls up **extra info** about the collectible – like **when it was acquired** and for how much.

<div class="blog_image_wrap blog_wide_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/400780400920/2/fJQPQ47AWWg.71776/27a7bbdff11001ec2b" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400920/2/fJQPQ47AWWg.71776/27a7bbdff11001ec2b" title="" alt="Collectibles on Your Profile" srcset="/file/400780400920/2/fJQPQ47AWWg.71776/27a7bbdff11001ec2b , 2x"></a></div>

> Collectibles can be bought and sold on the [Fragment](https://fragment.com/) platform, which uses the **TON blockchain**.

#### Share Location Indefinitely

When meeting up with friends or taking a trip, you can **share your live location** with individual users or entire **group chats**. This lets others follow your journey – or even [set an alert](https://t.me/TelegramTips/117) for when you arrive.

Now you can **add more time** to an existing shared location. And if you want friends and family to keep track of your movements for extended periods, there is a new option to share your location with them indefinitely, **until you turn it off**.

> To start sharing your location, open the attachment menu in a private chat or a group and select _Location > Share My Live Location for…_.

#### Reaction Notification Settings

We've added new settings that let you **control notifications** for reactions to **your messages**.

<div class="blog_image_wrap blog_wide_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/400780400329/2/tJfujGKF3qs.154708/d6677bb244826fb389" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400444/2/QUk5NnPw9VI.70569/25b44982d898332d19" title="" alt="Reaction Notification Settings" srcset="/file/400780400329/2/tJfujGKF3qs.154708/d6677bb244826fb389 , 2x"></a></div>

This update also introduces notifications about **reactions to your stories**, so you will never miss a heart again. (Or a flame. Or a thumbs up. Or a… pigeon? Whatever that means.)

> The new settings are available in _Settings > Notifications and Sounds > Reactions_.

#### Profile Pictures in Forwarded Messages

Forwarded messages now show the **profile picture** of the user from whom they were forwarded. This makes them **more recognizable** in the chat – and makes it easier to read forwarded conversations with **multiple authors**.

<div class="blog_image_wrap blog_wide_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/400780400689/2/zAJNHqhmM1s.161959/a3f36157698253839b" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400689/2/zAJNHqhmM1s.161959/a3f36157698253839b" title="" alt="Profile Pictures in Forwarded Messages" srcset="/file/400780400689/2/zAJNHqhmM1s.161959/a3f36157698253839b , 2x"></a></div>

If you limit who can [link back to your account](https://t.me/TelegramTips/183) when forwarding, your profile picture **will not appear** on messages forwarded from you.

> **Tip:** On Telegram, you can set a separate [public photo](/blog/hidden-media-zero-storage-profile-pics/#public-profile-pictures) that will be displayed to users you don't know.

Premium users can now include **animated** and **custom emoji** in polls. At long last, democracy can help decide whether animated ducks are better than static cats.

#### Mass Moderation for Groups

Group admins can now select several messages and perform **multiple moderation actions** at the same time – in a new menu with **individual toggles** for each user and action to maximize efficiency.

The menu also has options to **restrict** user permissions instead of banning them from the community right away.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400609/5/4UVyF51I1og.97989/caeab42fb3ca75156a" style="max-width: 600px;" title="" alt="Mass Moderation for Groups"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400131/4/abXCUuoeP3Q.7742521.mp4/0cf13118b00b906144" type="video/mp4"></video></div>

> Admins of groups with **200+ members** can also enable [aggressive spam filtering](https://t.me/TelegramTips/365) to automatically detect and restrict problematic users in their chat.

##### Improved Recent Actions

The _Recent Actions_ section lets group admins view **notable events** that took place in the chat in the past 48 hours, like deleted or edited messages and admin activity.

With this update, recent actions are now **more compact**, with similar actions by the same admin **grouped together**. Admins can also see **replies** and to which **topics** recent action messages belong.

<div class="blog_image_wrap blog_wide_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/400780400989/7/KXLeUl-mCsE.116704/8d46e8be130ae5e348" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400989/7/KXLeUl-mCsE.116704/8d46e8be130ae5e348" title="" alt="Improved Recent Actions" srcset="/file/400780400989/7/KXLeUl-mCsE.116704/8d46e8be130ae5e348 , 2x"></a></div>

> We also fixed text highlighting when you search recent actions – now it works just as well as when you search in regular Telegram chats.

#### Reaction Management for Channels

Channel admins now have an extra way to **manage reactions** – selecting a **maximum number** of reaction _types_ each post can have to keep things tidy.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400842/2/qoOKc_PYptw.132455/305a6cbe95cebc21d7" style="max-width: 600px;" title="" alt="Reaction Management for Channels"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400778/3/72NUYs6r_5E.1169331.mp4/5f947b6a506796ac46" type="video/mp4"></video></div>

> This allows channels to **frequently change** their available reactions _(like adding something relevant for a holiday)_, without a bunch of Easter bunnies popping up on past posts.

##### <img class="icon" src="https://telegram.org/file/464001305/110b0/-bpC5P3HJp4.6317/a2b79589744c739331" alt="Premium:"> Unhide Ads

By default, subscribing to Telegram Premium **hides ads in channels**. If you wish to still see them – either to **check your own ads** or help channels [earn rewards](#rewards-for-creators), there is a new toggle to **show sponsored messages** in _Settings > Telegram Business_.

#### <img class="icon" src="https://telegram.org/file/464001305/110b0/-bpC5P3HJp4.6317/a2b79589744c739331" alt="Premium:"> Open Stories Anonymously

While viewing stories, Premium users can activate **Stealth Mode** in the story menu to **hide their recent views** and continue hiding views for the **next 25 minutes**.

Now you can also activate Stealth Mode **from the chat list** – simply **press and hold** a story in your feed and select _'View Anonymously'_. This opens the story **without adding you** to the list of viewers even for a second, and also turns on Stealth Mode.

#### Swipe for Next Topic

When browsing groups that have [topics](/blog/topics-in-groups-collectible-usernames/#topics-in-groups) enabled, you can **pull up** at the bottom of the chat to go to the next unread topic.

<div class="blog_image_wrap blog_wide_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/400780400584/2/yLhbAe6F5T4.96247/a0e9472b98c03f3c08" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400584/2/yLhbAe6F5T4.96247/a0e9472b98c03f3c08" title="" alt="Swipe for Next Topic" srcset="/file/400780400584/2/yLhbAe6F5T4.96247/a0e9472b98c03f3c08 , 2x"></a></div>

#### Instant View on Telegram Desktop

Telegram's cross-platform [Desktop app](https://desktop.telegram.org/) now supports Instant View for websites – opening the page and **all its media** directly in your app with **zero load times**.

<div class="blog_image_wrap blog_wide_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/400780400482/1/hsJhUcJWY4A.683289/64f3f91e190a14f663" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400482/1/hsJhUcJWY4A.683289/64f3f91e190a14f663" title="" alt="Instant View on Telegram Desktop" srcset="/file/400780400482/1/hsJhUcJWY4A.683289/64f3f91e190a14f663 , 2x"></a></div>

> If you ever find errors on an Instant View page, scroll to the bottom and tap _'Wrong Layout?'_ to send us feedback.

#### Rewards for Creators

A new [reward system](/blog/monetization-for-channels/) has launched for channel owners – sharing **50%** of the revenue from ads shown in their channels. Withdrawals on [Fragment](https://fragment.com/) are **already available** for eligible channels – with **no fees**.

> Users can **promote their channel** or bot by creating sponsored messages on the [Telegram Ad Platform](https://ads.telegram.org/) – powered by the **TON blockchain**.
