# AI-Powered Sticker Search, Improved Videos and More

Today's update lets you search **millions of stickers** with AI, copy video links **at the current time**, set **cover photos** for videos, react with Stars **as your channel**, browse **similar bots** — and more.

#### AI-Powered Search for Millions of Stickers

In December, we added [AI-powered search](/blog/affiliate-programs-ai-sticker-search/#ai-powered-sticker-search) for **over 40,000 stickers** and emoji from Telegram's **official packs**.

With this update, the AI model now returns results from **millions of custom stickers** and emoji uploaded by Telegram users. Try searching for complex keywords like `thinking monkey` or `space dog` – the sticker panel will instantly return exactly what you're looking for. This feature supports searching in **29 languages**, including English, Spanish, Arabic and Hindi.

#### Copy Video Link at Current Time

Since [2019](/evolution/#august-2019), captions and replies to video messages have **supported timestamps** like `1:38` to highlight funny moments or direct viewers to certain sections.

When sharing a link to a video on Telegram in chats or **on other platforms**, you can now include the current timestamp **in the link**. When someone taps on the link, it will open the video in Telegram to that **exact moment**.

> To share a link to a video, tap the **share arrow** in the video player. It will automatically give you the option to include the **current time** in the link.

#### Video Covers in Channels

Like videos you add [to your story](/blog/w3-browser-mini-app-store/#story-covers), you can now choose a frame to use as a **cover photo** for videos you post in **channels**. To create exciting thumbnails, use the video editor to **add text**, stickers, emoji and more before selecting a cover.

> To select a video cover, tap the three-dot icon in the top corner of the video editor and select _'Edit Cover'_.

#### Saved Progress for Videos

Videos you watch now automatically **save your progress** — so you can take a break at any time and return exactly where you left off.

> All videos on Telegram support [picture-in-picture playback](/blog/notifications-bots/#improved-picture-in-picture-on-android) so you can **keep watching** while you reply to messages and browse other chats.

#### React with Stars as a Channel

Users can [support content creators with Stars](/blog/superchannels-star-reactions-subscriptions/#star-reactions) by sending the **Star reaction** to their channel posts. To provide greater privacy and allow content creators to **promote their channel** in the leaderboard, users can now react with Stars as **one of their channels**.

#### Similar Bots

Like with [channels](/blog/similar-channels/#similar-channels), on bot profiles you can now see **a list of similar bots** — to help you find other **popular services**, utilities and games.
