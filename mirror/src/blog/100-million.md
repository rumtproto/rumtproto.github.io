# 100,000,000 Monthly Active Users

<img src="https://telegram.org/img/tl_card_wecandoit.gif" class="blog_side_image">

Two and a half years ago we launched Telegram. Then you told your friends and co-workers about it. And they told their friends and co-workers. And we started growing.

Now Telegram has more than **100,000,000** monthly active users. **350,000** new users sign up each day. We're delivering [**15 billion**](/blog/15-billion/) messages daily.

Thank you for spreading the word, keep it up!
