# Live Stories, Repeated Messages, Auctions for Gifts and More

In today's update, you can **start a stream** with a **live chat** in your Telegram Story. We've also added **repeating scheduled messages**, an **auction system** for new gifts that ensures **fair distribution** — and more.

#### Live Stories

Every Telegram user can now **host live streams** in Telegram Stories to **share experiences** in real time.

Anyone watching the stream can **send comments** just like in [group calls and video chats](/blog/comments-in-video-chats-threads-for-bots/). To **support content creators** and make your comment stand out, **highlight and pin** your message with Telegram Stars by tapping the **message bubble icon**.

The Star icon in the bottom corner of the stream shows **how many Stars** the host has earned so far — and viewers can **tap the icon** to donate additional Stars.

> Live Stories can be launched from **user accounts**, as well as **groups and channels** that have [unlocked posting Stories](/blog/channel-stories/#stories-for-channels) via boosts. Live Stories offer the same privacy controls as ordinary stories: _Everyone_, _My Contacts_, _Close Friends_ and _Selected Users_.

#### Hosting Live Stories

Streams in stories support **RTMP keys** for external apps like OBS and XSplit — which allows content creators to **seamlessly stream** on Telegram with their existing setup.

> To manage comments or set a **price per comment**, tap in the top corner while streaming and select _'Live Settings'_. To make a user a chat admin, give them the **admin permission** _'Manage Video Chats / Live Streams'_ in your group or channel.

#### Repeated Messages

Scheduled messages and reminders can now be **set to repeat** at intervals like **every day** or **twice per month** — to **automate tasks** at work or **build healthy habits**.

> To schedule a message, press and hold the _'Send'_ arrow **in any chat**. To make it repeat, tap _'Repeat'_ and select your preferred interval.

#### Auctions for New Gifts

**Telegram Gifts** are [unique collectibles](/blog/collectible-gifts-and-more/#collectible-gifts) which you can [wear](/blog/wear-gifts-blockchain-and-more/) in your status, [group](/blog/post-search-story-albums-and-more/#gift-collections) into collections, [move](/blog/wear-gifts-blockchain-and-more/#move-gifts-to-the-blockchain) to your blockchain wallet — or [trade](/blog/gift-marketplace-and-more/) on the [Marketplace](/blog/gift-marketplace-and-more/). **Limited-edition gifts** sometimes sell out in [just a few seconds](https://t.me/durov/446).

To guarantee fair distribution, **new gift releases** will be based on **auctions** — allowing users to **place bids with Stars** for new gifts over **several rounds**.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400578/2/d3C1ElKJauw.67230.jpg/dbbb926cf801012a47" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Auctions for new gifts"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400784/3/xq1vth1xb9I.3560019.mp4/0f263a2fe2f57925e1" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

> Your place in the auction determines the **unique number** of your gift — so the **top bidder** in Round 1 will receive the **#1** gift, 2nd place will get **#2**, and so on.

At the end of each round, the top bidders will each receive a gift — while lower bids **automatically carry over** to the next round. Once all the gifts have been awarded, any remaining bids will be **automatically refunded**.

You can participate more than once to get **several gifts** — and increase your chances by **increasing your bid** at any time. The bid interface shows **how many Stars** you need to bid to win a gift in that round.

> To browse gifts for sale, go to _Settings > Send a Gift_.

Gift auctions show an **'Auction'** banner and have dedicated `t.me/auction` [links](https://t.me/auction/TelegramPin). The next auction is coming very soon, [stay tuned](https://t.me/durov)!
