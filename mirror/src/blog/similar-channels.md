# Similar Channels, Reposting Stories, and 9 More Features

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400728/3/BHHImkTAW8k.135826.webp/9b1105a38732bf89f9" srcset="/file/400780400221/5/dG9oweNF4IA.433382.webp/61c91e8e6f5d73ad29, 1200w" title="I had neither friend nor channel on Telegram, and was therefore as free as air — or as free as an inbox of eleven messages and six stories a day will permit a man to be..." alt="Similar Channels, Reposting Stories, and 9 More Features">](https://telegram.org/file/400780400221/5/dG9oweNF4IA.433382.webp/61c91e8e6f5d73ad29)

Today's update lets you **find similar channels** to those you follow, **repost stories** from friends and channels, and **add video messages** to stories. We've also added **profile colors**, **wallpapers for both sides**, **story stats** and **custom reactions** for channels, **voice transcription** for everyone and _much_ more.

#### Similar Channels

Hundreds of millions follow Telegram channels to keep up with latest news, entertainment content and AI-generated pictures of six-fingered cats.

To make discovering channels easier, when you **join a channel**, you'll now see a list of **similar public channels**.

Only **public channels** appear in the list, they are selected automatically based on similarities in their **subscriber bases**.

> You can also access **Similar Channels** from a channel's profile.

#### Reposting Stories

You can **repost** stories from your friends and favorite channels to your page, increasing their audience in just two taps.

Reposting a story lets you **add extra content** like text, audio or even a [video comment](#video-messages-on-stories) – along with a separate **visibility setting** for your repost.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400586/2/rsxHM2T0Xmc.68360/893dad8624255170ca" style="max-width: 600px;" title="Also introducing 'Repossessing Stories': simply repost one of your stories that has been reposted by someone else." alt="Reposting Stories"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400437/1/owwrdORabY0.8160338.mp4/d73ec8cd9635f4de3f" type="video/mp4"></video></div>

> To repost a story, tap the **share arrow** and select _'Repost Story'_ – to **protect privacy**, only stories that are visible to _'Everyone'_ can be reposted.

#### Video Messages on Stories

When posting a story, you can now add a **video message** to supply commentary or simply make a face. Video messages can be **resized** and moved around the screen, as well as along the time axis to **start or end** at any frame. The creative possibilities are endless — from giving backstory to a photo to creating a **video conversation** with yourself as different characters.

<div class="blog_image_wrap blog_wide_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/400780400640/1/0h303KgvGW0.110659/081aab1f4a9491df4d" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400640/1/0h303KgvGW0.110659/081aab1f4a9491df4d" title="For the inevitable 'That's All, Folks!' moment." alt="Video Messages on Stories" srcset="/file/400780400640/1/0h303KgvGW0.110659/081aab1f4a9491df4d , 2x"></a></div>

Stories you **repost** also support video messages – so you can share your thoughts on a trending topic or join a conga line of creators reposting each other.

> To record a video message, hold the **camera icon** in the story editor. Hold on the video track at the bottom of the screen to control **volume**.

#### <img class="icon" src="https://telegram.org/file/464001305/110b0/-bpC5P3HJp4.6317/a2b79589744c739331" alt="Premium:"> Profile Colors

Our [previous update](/blog/reply-revolution/#account-colors) let Premium users **choose a color** for their name, links they send and replies to their messages.

Starting today, Telegram Premium also lets you set a **unique color combination** with **logos** (like [these](https://t.me/addemoji/MovieIcons) or [these](https://t.me/addemoji/GameIcons)) for **your profile** to make the page stand out.

> To customize your messages or profile, go to _Settings > Chat Settings > Change Your Color_ on Android. On iOS, it's under _Settings > Appearance > Your Color_.

#### <img class="icon" src="https://telegram.org/file/464001305/110b0/-bpC5P3HJp4.6317/a2b79589744c739331" alt="Premium:"> Wallpapers for Both Sides

Anyone can set custom wallpapers for **individual chats** – making each conversation easy to recognize with **different themes** and your **favorite photos**.

Now, Premium users can set a wallpaper for **both sides**, applying the same appearance for both themselves and their chat partner.

> To set a custom wallpaper in any **1-on-1 chat**, tap ⋮ (on iOS, open the user's profile, then select ⋯) _\> Change Wallpaper_.

#### Voice-to-Text for Everyone

[Since 2022](/blog/700-million-and-premium/#voice-to-text), Premium users have been able to **transcribe voice and video messages** – helping them quickly see what was said or even **translate audio** in different languages.

With this update, **all users** can convert up to **2 messages** per week into text – simply tap the **→A icon** to try it out.

> **Note:** This feature will be rolling out **gradually** and may not be available everywhere immediately.

> Telegram Premium still allows for **unlimited** voice-to-text conversion.

#### Story Stats for Channels

Channels that are able to [post stories](/blog/channel-stories/#stories-for-channels) can now **see statistics** for **story views**, **shares** and **reactions** – with **detailed graphs** to track their reach and performance.

> Story stats can be seen by tapping ⋮/⋯ when viewing your story, or in _Channel Info >_ ⋮/⋯ _> Statistics_.

**Channel stats**, boosts and giveaway settings are **easier to reach** via _Channel Info > Edit > Statistics and Boosts_.

#### Custom Reactions for Channels

Channel admins have a new interface for **managing reactions** – where they can add or remove specific emoji.

In addition to standard reactions, channels can also add **custom emoji** – getting **1** custom reaction for each [level](/blog/channel-stories/#stories-for-channels) they have.

#### Code Highlighting in Messages

Thanks to the winner of a [recent coding contest](https://t.me/contest/353), all Telegram apps are now able to **detect the coding language** and **highlight the syntax** in messages sent with **\`\`\`code formatting\`\`\`** – so you can finally see the difference between Java and C#. <img class="emoji" src="https://telegram.org/img/emoji/40/F09F9889.png" width="20" height="20" alt="😉">

#### Improvements for Topics

In groups with [Topics](/blog/topics-in-groups-collectible-usernames/#topics-in-groups) enabled, your app will **save your view preference** of _View as Messages_ or _View as Topics_ when you open the group again.
