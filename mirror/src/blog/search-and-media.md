# In-App Media Playback and Search in Chats

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140255/1/BbzDx4qIQ60/7dc14c9f68f8f1ec3e" class="blog_side_image">

The new version of Telegram allows you to play media from services like YouTube, Vimeo or SoundCloud directly from Telegram – without having to leave the app. Just tap on the preview of a link to an external media to view the video or listen to the audio file (speaking of audio files, they are now beautifully rendered and played in chats).

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140106/2/mVMXO-QaxKY/334ae516cf90b689ef" title="In-app YouTube video on iOS" style="max-height: 320px; padding: 10px 5px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140106/2/mVMXO-QaxKY/334ae516cf90b689ef) [<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140675/2/wRoIASj9RNI/fcfa783056834a8637" title="In-app Soundcloud playback on Android" style="max-height: 320px; padding: 10px 5px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140675/2/wRoIASj9RNI/fcfa783056834a8637) [<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140379/1/QqGC6AxpGCA/d16e154f800b529ca9" title="In-app Vimeo video on Android" style="max-height: 320px; padding: 10px 5px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140379/1/QqGC6AxpGCA/d16e154f800b529ca9)  
  

The new attachment menu in Android is not only gorgeous and beautifully animated; it also allows you to share contacts, audios and recent photos way easier:

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140955/3/uL7GVV0tAVo/e3ad6d760787d22d4a" title="The new attachment menu on Android" style="max-height: 320px; padding: 10px 5px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140955/3/uL7GVV0tAVo/e3ad6d760787d22d4a)  

All of your communications in Telegram are already instantly searchable with the [multi-purpose search field](/blog/shared-files/#multisearch), but now you can also search for messages in specific chats. Just tap on the name of the chat in iOS or toggle the Actions menu in Android:

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140526/1/MGlt5-tetT4/b6cf38717c32f3eb71" title="On iOS, tap the header, then 'Search'" style="max-height: 330px; padding: 10px 5px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140526/1/MGlt5-tetT4/b6cf38717c32f3eb71) [<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140363/1/EHNWdQDBgw4/7c7a2ee0da9dfd0a2c" title="Use the arrows to navigate between matches" style="max-height: 330px; padding: 10px 5px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140363/1/EHNWdQDBgw4/7c7a2ee0da9dfd0a2c) [<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140207/1/_2sA3cqdcXM/ba145a87cf59c6f3b0" title="Looks pretty much the same on Android" style="max-height: 330px; padding: 10px 5px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140207/1/_2sA3cqdcXM/ba145a87cf59c6f3b0)  
  

You will see the number of occurrences of the keyword in the chat and will be able to navigate through them with the up/down arrows. Enjoy!
