# Telegram for Android Wear 2.0

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140743/4/8KTqfwpP2Xg.30391/8973f93bfd547e4d41" class="blog_side_image">

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140276/3/HGDK8FLA0z4.113346/7ee3ecb54ecf0e501b" title="Telegram launches Android Wear 2.0 app">](https://telegram.org/file/811140276/3/HGDK8FLA0z4.113346/7ee3ecb54ecf0e501b)

Google has just unveiled [Android Wear 2.0](https://blog.google/products/android-wear/android-wear-20-make-most-every-minute/), the latest in smartwatch platforms. It supports standalone apps, so today we're releasing **Telegram for Android Wear 2.0.** It‘s everything you’d want from a perfect watch app — compact, beautiful, and super-fast.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 640px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="YYY" style="max-width: 640px;"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/811140037/1/CoPFO1iMFu8.4014450.mp4/857242466200a07c25" type="video/mp4"></video></div>

Using this tiny yet powerful app, you can browse your chats and quickly reply to messages with voice, text, emoji, or stickers. You can also view your contacts and even create groups right from your watch.

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140855/2/QhPFxtrKQQU.204643/9a1dd613ab3f0a6307" title="Send voice, text messages, and stickers">](https://telegram.org/file/811140855/2/QhPFxtrKQQU.204643/9a1dd613ab3f0a6307)

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140090/3/R2GWTvcOCto.156067/b322ddc88a5f3275d7" title="Browse your chats and get notifications">](https://telegram.org/file/811140090/3/R2GWTvcOCto.156067/b322ddc88a5f3275d7)

The smartwatch app also supports several themes in different colors. By the way, theme support is coming to our main Android app soon, so stay tuned.

Telegram for Android Wear 2.0 is available for download on Google Play. If you have Android Wear 2.0 installed on your watch, just search for Telegram in the store.
