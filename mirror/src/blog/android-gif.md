# GIF and Image Search on Android

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140561/1/xqLpaUYxq10/9b6056d4a22958d506" class="blog_side_image">

One of the best beloved [features](/blog/gifs/) of our iOS app finally comes to [Telegram for Android](https://telegram.org/dl/android). You can now easily search the web for GIFs and images and send them to your friends.

To do this, simply open the **Gallery** attachment menu — you will find the new **Search Web** and **Search GIF** tabs at the top.

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140898/1/K8hRfYJFEsc/f9dd927bf146f8bf99" title="New GIF and Image Search tabs" style="padding: 10px 5px; width: 195px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140898/1/K8hRfYJFEsc/f9dd927bf146f8bf99) [<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140332/1/xlFuhKpc5t0/df8c142e6ae51b83f7" title="GIF search" style="padding: 10px 5px; width: 195px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140332/1/xlFuhKpc5t0/df8c142e6ae51b83f7) [<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140197/1/87ffI8QnY9U/b9d8d9ab02d536dc30" title="Preview GIF" style="padding: 10px 5px; width: 195px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140197/1/87ffI8QnY9U/b9d8d9ab02d536dc30)

Your recent gifs and images will be saved, so you can send them again with just a few taps. This is very useful when you're out of words and emoji are just not enough.

  

Stay tuned for more updates this year!
