# Gifts, Verification Platform and More

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400621/2/yRMlI8_RUPM.41567.png/98998d5293f1576d10">

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400090/6/LdFibrvV600.135656.webp/60d103ac455c997597" srcset="/file/400780400889/7/g7no8X5pdTA.399616.webp/da8a0fae9c8ea6b980, 1200w" title="" alt="Introducing Gifts, Verification Platform and More">](https://telegram.org/file/400780400889/7/g7no8X5pdTA.399616.webp/da8a0fae9c8ea6b980)

Users can now send **Gifts** to their friends. The recipients of gifts can display them on their **profile pages** or turn them into **Stars**. Telegram Stars can be used for many things, including [supporting creators](/blog/mini-app-bar-paid-media-and-more/#pay-for-content-with-telegram-stars) and buying services in [mini apps](/blog/telegram-stars/).

Additionally, Telegram launched a platform for businesses to verify customer **phone numbers**: this new verification platform lets users easily confirm their phone numbers by receiving a code via Telegram and will cost businesses just **$0.01** per user. We've also improved **video chats**, added new ways to **report content** on Telegram — and more.

#### Gifts

Telegram users can now send each other **gifts** for [Telegram Stars](/blog/telegram-stars/) — celebrating holidays and congratulating contacts with **animated artwork** and **custom messages**.

When you receive a gift, you can **display it** on the new _'Gifts'_ tab of your profile — or discard the gift and get some of its Stars to spend elsewhere, including on more gifts.

> To send a gift, open the user's profile and tap ⋮ / ⋯ _\> Send a Gift_. You can add any message or emoji.

When sending a gift, you can choose to **hide your name**. The recipient will still know who sent it, but nobody else will see your name if the gift is displayed on their profile.

#### Verification Platform

We know from our own experience how expensive it can be for businesses to **verify phone numbers** of their customers. Starting today, any business, app or website can send **verification codes** via Telegram and pay for them on [Fragment](https://fragment.com). Telegram codes are **faster**, **cheaper**, **more secure** and **convenient** than SMS or other alternatives.

Users will **instantly receive** messages with codes in a **special chat** inside Telegram.

> Telegram's Gateway API is completely **free to test**. Interested developers can learn more in our [detailed guide](/gateway/).

#### Improved Reporting Interface

Each day, Telegram moderators remove millions of pieces of content that violates our [Terms of Service](/tos/). Since 2015, all Telegram apps allow users to **report messages** to moderators.

To further improve moderators' ability to process reports at scale and our **AI capabilities**, the reporting menu has been expanded with even more **detailed options** for each category. The list of reporting reasons is now dynamically updated from the server, allowing us to suggest the most relevant reporting reasons in real-time, adapting to unfolding events and trends.

#### Redesigned Video Chats on iOS

Video chats on iOS have been **rebuilt from scratch** to increase performance and **improve battery life**.

This also means that devices now stay **dramatically cooler** on long calls — even with **multiple video feeds** from dozens of participants.

#### RTMP Streams on Android

Telegram for Android now has support for **livestreaming with RTMP** — allowing you to **generate a stream key** and connect your video feed to **popular apps** like OBS Studio and XSplit Broadcaster.
