# Music on Profiles, Stickers Mini App, New Profiles, and More

In today’s update, you can **add music** to your profile, choose a default **profile tab**, set **unique chat themes** based on your collectible gifts, help your friends **upgrade their gifts** — and more.

#### Music On Your Profile

Audio files from any of your chats can now be **added to your profile** with a single tap in the media player. Once on your profile, the track appears in a **stylish bar** beneath your photo.

As you **add tracks** to your profile, it automatically **creates a playlist** that includes the previous ones.

> To add music to your profile, tap the _'Add to Profile'_ button in the **media player** or select that track and tap _Save to… > Profile_.

#### Stickers Mini App

Telegram users can **create custom stickers** with a [built-in editor](/blog/sticker-maker/) and manage packs through the [@Stickers](http://t.me/Stickers) bot.

Starting today, the new [Stickers Mini App](https://t.me/stickers?startapp) lets you **create stickers and emoji**, manage your packs, and view **detailed usage statistics**.

> Any user can create, send and **share custom artwork** using Telegram's [open platform](/stickers/) for stickers and emoji.

#### New Profile Design on Android

Thanks to a [recent contest](https://t.me/contest/420), profiles on Android now feature a **sleek new design** with **scrolling animations** first introduced on iOS.

#### Default Profile Tab

Now you can **choose which tab** appears first when someone **opens your profile** — to better highlight your **saved stories** or your **gift collections**.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400703/3/vBVspVxDWHU.44173.jpg/729cd5ca06efd59757" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Default Profile Tab"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400616/3/Z09Nr6-qp5M.983096.mp4/20d09b8af92fc03113" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

> To change the default tab on your profile, go to _Settings > My Profile_, hold down on a tab and select _Set as Main Tab_.

#### Gift Themes

Since 2021, Telegram users can apply themes to [individual chats](/blog/chat-themes-interactive-emoji-read-receipts/#chat-themes). Today we are adding dozens of new **dynamic chat wallpapers** based on Telegram gifts — which their owners can now set as a **custom theme** in any private chat.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400880/4/ynbL0oYjVgw.58786.jpg/a5b2e0b572cb8c2ce7" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Gift Themes"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400631/4/M8a8LgVIhEg.2206403.mp4/f36da56259eba1d9b7" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

The only way to unlock these wallpapers is by **owning a gift**. Each wallpaper is based on **gift attributes**, using the model and backdrop to create a **unique theme** for every collectible. One gift unlocks one theme for one chat.

> This currently works with [Plush Pepes](https://fragment.com/gifts/plushpepe), [Precious Peaches](https://fragment.com/gifts/preciouspeach), [Durov's Caps](https://fragment.com/gifts/durovscap) and [Heart Lockets](https://fragment.com/gifts/heartlocket).

#### Upgrading Gifts for Other Users

You can now send Stars to cover the cost of upgrading a gift on **someone else's profile** — in case they're low on Stars or didn't know it could be converted. If the user later decides to upgrade their gift, this will be **free** for them.

The new **'Upgradable' filter** filter makes it easy to find gifts that can be upgraded. And with the **'Upgrade Next Gift'** button, you can upgrade multiple gifts back-to-back.

To make unboxing collectibles even more exciting, our designers added **extra suspense** to the upgrade animation.

#### Gift Value

Users can **securely trade collectible gifts** in the [Gift Marketplace](/blog/gift-marketplace-and-more/#buy-rare-gifts-in-the-marketplace) within Telegram.

When viewing a gift's attributes, the new _'Value'_ field shows its **approximate resale price**. You can expand it to view more **statistics** like the **average sale price** of other gifts in the same collection.
