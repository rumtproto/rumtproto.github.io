# Custom Sticker Sets

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140219/2/RlVQPk2jcUo/2173062bb911aca8d9" class="blog_side_image">

> [Check out Pavel's Channel for some cool sticker sets »](https://telegram.me/durov/40)

Starting today, artists can submit their custom sticker sets via the Telegram [@stickers](https://telegram.me/stickers) bot. Each sticker set gets a **permanent link**, so that users can easily add new stickers and share them with friends.

This enables artists of the world to unleash their creativity and help Telegram users customize their experience. Our in-house artist (responsible for the graphic art in this blog) created the **Animals** set as an example. If you have Telegram installed and updated, this link will open the set: [](https://telegram.me/addstickers/Animals)[https://telegram.me/addstickers/Animals](https://telegram.me/addstickers/Animals).

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140202/1/6x4PE8Ay684/bff1728992c9c639d3" title="Sticker set preview" style="width: 195px; padding: 10px 5px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140202/1/6x4PE8Ay684/bff1728992c9c639d3)  
  

Independent designers already began submitting their sticker sets, and we notice stickers like [Flags of the World](https://telegram.me/addstickers/Flags) or [Vicky](https://telegram.me/addstickers/Vicky) uploaded to the [@stickers](https://telegram.me/stickers) bot.

#### Sharing Stickers

Sending a link isn‘t the only way to share a sticker set. When you receive a sticker from a set you do not currently have installed, simply tap on it and choose ’Add to Stickers‘ (’Info' on iOS) to preview and add the whole set.

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140970/1/bCNTkkSmgfo/eb30a50d4ff76e81c2" title="Tap on a sticker to add a new set" style="width: 195px; padding: 10px 5px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140970/1/bCNTkkSmgfo/eb30a50d4ff76e81c2) [<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140461/1/GJVav5OsVws/f7ccb954c24755a2a8" title="Preview and add sticker set" style="width: 195px; padding: 10px 5px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140461/1/GJVav5OsVws/f7ccb954c24755a2a8)  
  

Once you've added a set, its stickers become available via [emoji tooltips](/blog/stickers/) or the dedicated sticker panel. The iOS app now has one too — you can manually switch it on/off in Chat Settings — Stickers.

The new **Stickers** menu allows you to view, uninstall and share your sticker sets.

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140196/1/au_8ExYtQc0/5a0a48375b897976b7" title="Manage your sticker sets from Chat Settings — Stickers" style="width: 195px; padding: 10px 5px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140196/1/au_8ExYtQc0/5a0a48375b897976b7)  
  

This update is available for [Android](https://telegram.org/dl/android) and [iOS](https://telegram.org/dl/ios) and is coming to other apps soon. Sticker sets on Telegram are now fully equipped to spread virally and devour the world.
