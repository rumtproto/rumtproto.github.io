# First BotPrize Winners Get &#036;200,000

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140614/2/flKQKZ7xUOE.27938.gif/5574a04570218c9e11" class="blog_side_image">

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140545/3/CtwAeY9PCBc.271011/d2704b332fb92de89d" title="Telegram Bot Prize">](https://telegram.org/file/811140545/3/CtwAeY9PCBc.271011/d2704b332fb92de89d)

Earlier this year we announced [BotPrize](/blog/botprize/), our initiative to award **$25,000-$50,000** grants to the creators of the best Telegram bots. Today we selected five bots for the first batch of grants, amounting to a total of over **$200,000**.

For our first pick of winners, we focused on bots that have already gained massive popularity in some markets. The prize money will help their developers create an even better experience for their existing and future users.

#### BotPrize: November 2016

In this batch, we awarded bots from five very different categories: Photo-editing, Productivity, Games, Dating, and Finance.

**Photo-editing**  
[@icon8bot](https://telegram.me/icon8bot) – an AI-based bot that allows you to apply great-looking filters to photos using neural networks and machine learning technology. More: [icon-ai.com](http://icon-ai.com/)

**Productivity**  
[@integram\_bot](https://telegram.me/integram_bot) – a bot that allows you to set up integrations with third-party services for developers such as Trello, Gitlab, Bitbucket, and more. More: [integram.com](https://integram.org/)

**Games**  
[@werewolfbot](https://telegram.me/werewolfbot) – a ‘Werewolf Game’ bot for group chats that gained popularity in SE Asia long before we launched our [gaming platform](/blog/games/) last month. More: [tgwerewolf.com](https://www.tgwerewolf.com/)

**Dating**  
[@strangerbot](https://telegram.me/strangerbot) – a bot that connects two random users to chat anonymously, which gained huge popularity after going viral in Southern Europe. More: [strangerbot.com](http://strangerbot.com/)

**Finance**  
[@octopocket\_bot](https://telegram.me/octopocket_bot) – a smart-wallet bot that allows users from the EU to transfer money to each other, and is integrated into the ATM system of Spain. More: [octopocket.com](https://octopocket.com/)

* * *

We will continue awarding creators of great bots throughout this year and the following year in **$200,000** batches.

Have fun using or building bots!
