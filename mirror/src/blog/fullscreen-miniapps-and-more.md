# Mini Apps 2.0: Full-Screen Mode, Home Screen Icons, Geolocation and 10 more features

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400659/2/0wC0J6QfOS0.22537.png/5fb9f786e4db70fc04">

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400735/3/bkmI2NPDTfg.131944.webp/1e2ab7ed8fd7bcd9b5" srcset="/file/400780400141/2/1pxhnZe0Tls.415926.webp/2f307b34a3bbe34dc3, 1200w" title="" alt="Mini Apps 2.0: Full-Screen Mode, Home Screen Icons, Geolocation and 10 more features">](https://telegram.org/file/400780400141/2/1pxhnZe0Tls.415926.webp/2f307b34a3bbe34dc3)

Today we launch the **largest update** in the history of mini apps. They get a full-screen mode, subscription plans, home screen shortcuts, sending gifts, sharing media, creating documents, access to geolocation, motion tracker, setting emoji statuses — and more.

#### Full-Screen Mode

Mini apps are now able to use the **entire screen** in portrait or **landscape orientation** — allowing apps to host **more game genres** with expanded gestures and interfaces.

#### Device Motion Tracking

Mini apps are able to receive information about the **motion** of your device to add **unique controls** to games or build **immersive VR experiences**.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400579/5/PfP8hFLhBv8.104935/03e0bc7d20d6ce4d98" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Device Motion Tracking"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400159/2/Kzdm3KQIU18.4723735.mp4/e112d04952765b971a" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

Developers can **lock** the screen orientation to ensure there is no unnecessary rotation if their app uses device motion for controls.

#### Home Screen Shortcuts

You can now place **direct shortcuts** to specific mini apps on the **home screen** of your device — accessing your favorite games and services in **one tap**.

#### Geolocation Access

You can now **share your location** with mini apps — which gives developers the ability to make **location-based games** with points of interest or **interactive maps** for events.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400471/4/NxjzOa07Eyc.38651/62c7f445e0e06a4452" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Geolocation Access"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400995/4/5X1SreBoHEY.1731682.mp4/0e2f79c91e61b21b58" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

> Access to location data is **disabled by default** — you must specifically give location permissions to each **individual mini app** with which you'd like to share this data.

#### Gifts from Apps

Apps can now use their stars to **send you gifts** as rewards to celebrate achievements.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400202/4/MSVgIAZ6_rI.103173/ae2e299f54527b86dc" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Gifts from Apps"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400812/2/d3vTWpiGxlY.1808151.mp4/df11d2454b136a0cf0" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

We've also added a new **privacy setting** that lets you control who can send you gifts that will **appear on your profile**.

#### Emoji Statuses from Apps

With this update, Telegram Premium users can **set emoji statuses** from inside mini apps, showing others that they're in a game <img class="emoji" src="https://telegram.org/img/emoji/40/F09F8EAE.png" width="20" height="20" alt="🎮"> or a taxi <img class="emoji" src="https://telegram.org/img/emoji/40/F09F9A95.png" width="20" height="20" alt="🚕">.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400234/2/uDKud852DCw.39458/e632391b3beadeff09" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Emoji Statuses from Apps"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400124/6/8n9m2O9VeUI.3087497.mp4/7cb84d10c693d53820" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

Mini apps can integrate APIs from **other services** — instantly updating a user's emoji status when they start playing music <img class="emoji" src="https://telegram.org/img/emoji/40/F09F8EB6.png" width="20" height="20" alt="🎶">, open a book <img class="emoji" src="https://telegram.org/img/emoji/40/F09F939A.png" width="20" height="20" alt="📚"> or turn on a movie <img class="emoji" src="https://telegram.org/img/emoji/40/F09F8DBF.png" width="20" height="20" alt="🍿">.

Apps with [location permissions](#geolocation-access) could also be used to **update your status** when you're at work <img class="emoji" src="https://telegram.org/img/emoji/40/F09F92BC.png" width="20" height="20" alt="💼"> or out of the office <img class="emoji" src="https://telegram.org/img/emoji/40/F09F8F96.png" width="20" height="20" alt="🏖">.

> Developers can also create their own [custom emoji packs](/stickers/#animated-stickers-and-emoji) to offer as status options, adding to the **viral potential** of mini apps.

#### Media Sharing

You can now share media directly from **mini apps** — sending **referral codes**, custom memes and more to **any chat**.

#### Creating Documents

Mini apps can also generate documents and files which you can download — such as AI-generated profile pictures or audio files.

#### Subscription Plans

Developers are able to offer **subscriptions** to their mini app using Telegram Stars — **monetizing their efforts** with multiple tiers of content and features.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400439/3/fsIQBVDv8iM.42498/fb93eac0593624c6ff" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Subscription Plans for Mini Apps"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400502/3/yVyvz2IBzwk.3397983.mp4/b34526a4a87a0e18cc" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

Telegram Stars earned from **digital products** and **subscriptions** can be used to [increase message limits](/blog/dynamic-video-quality-and-more/#increased-message-limits-for-bots) for the app or [claim rewards](/blog/mini-app-bar-paid-media-and-more/#toncoin-rewards).

> Developers are also eligible to **earn rewards** from Telegram Ads in [bots and mini apps](/blog/dynamic-video-quality-and-more/#telegram-ads-in-bots).

#### Loading Screen Customization

Developers can further customize the **loading screen** of their mini app in [@BotFather](https://t.me/botfather) — adding **their own icon** and **specific colors** that will be displayed before the app starts loading.

#### Device Hardware Info

To better **optimize graphics** and **increase performance**, mini apps can access **basic hardware info** about your device, such as its processing power and memory capacity — then **automatically adjust** settings to provide the smoothest experience.

#### And More

Developers can now know when their mini app is minimized and restored from the [app bar](/blog/mini-app-bar-paid-media-and-more/#mini-app-bar) and make sure they handle transitions correctly.

> If you are a developer, see [this page](/bots/api-changelog/#november-17-2024) for detailed documentation and the full list of changes, including an easier way for mini apps to access profile pictures and support for third-party validation of mini app data.

Everyone can already try out early implementations of some of the new features in apps like [@playdeckbot](https://t.me/playdeckbot), [@tverse](https://t.me/tverse) and [@major](https://t.me/major) (the _Major Maze_ game on the _Games_ tab).

That's all for today's update. We're already working on the next big thing for mini apps and beyond.
