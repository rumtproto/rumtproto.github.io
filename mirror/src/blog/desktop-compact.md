# Telegram Desktop Adds Compact Mode

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140246/1/yhgI610ZZuY/bdad6f62df8496c3fa" class="blog_side_image">

The official Telegram app for [Windows, OSX and Linux](https://desktop.telegram.org) is now available in compact mode — you can resize the window so that only a single column remains. This makes it easier to use Telegram on smaller **desktop** or **laptop** screens.

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140940/2/WeoCQAS6TS0/f748d2ecb1e1b5285a" title="Chats view" style="padding: 10px 20px; width: 280px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140940/2/WeoCQAS6TS0/f748d2ecb1e1b5285a) [<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140661/1/l-zIUkBmcsk/b67952a55549d4aceb" title="Conversation view" style="padding: 10px 20px; width: 280px">](https://telegram.org/file/811140661/1/l-zIUkBmcsk/b67952a55549d4aceb)

You can download Telegram Desktop at [**desktop.telegram.org**](https://desktop.telegram.org). As of last week, this app is also available in the [Mac AppStore](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/telegram-desktop/id946399090), alongside with our [native OSX app](https://telegram.org/dl/osx).

To log in on your computer, simply enter your phone number and get a code delivered via Telegram to your phone. All your messages (except those from Secret Chats) are instantly **synced** between all your devices, so you can continue right where you left off.
