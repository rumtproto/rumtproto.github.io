# Public Post Search, Story Albums, Gift Collections and More

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400127/6/bX-EKwrq1iI.137960.webp/f896948d957d577376" srcset="/file/400780400445/2/tW7uey8zRqc.386416.webp/d6aa85485d834eebf1, 1200w" title="" alt="Public Post Search, Story Albums, Gift Collections and More">](https://telegram.org/file/400780400445/2/tW7uey8zRqc.386416.webp/d6aa85485d834eebf1)

Today's update introduces **global search** for **public posts** on Telegram, **sorting stories** into albums and **organizing gifts** into collections, **managing bots** via a new stylish mini app — and so much more.

#### Public Post Search

Search now has a _'Posts'_ tab, where you can see relevant results from **public channels**. This makes it even easier to find **specific information** and browse a **variety of sources** in one **lightning-fast** interface.

> Post search is initially available to **Telegram Premium** users — the people who help keep Telegram **free, fast and secure** for more than a **billion users** around the world.

#### Story Albums

Your favorite vacation stories, pet pictures and more can now be **organized into story albums** on your profile — to **create highlight reels** of your best adventures and favorite memes.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400250/4/xUj2HLLZevE.52750.jpg/bbbede90be19ea50e6" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Story Albums"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400830/4/4d3BQhtTJTU.2528777.mp4/e3785c56dc5c113545" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

**Businesses** and content creators who post stories **in their channel** can also utilize albums to **display products** for customers or **group content** into playlists.

> To add stories to an album, go to the _'Posts'_ tab in your profile or channel and tap _'Add Album'_.

#### Gift Collections

You can now group gifts on your profile or channel into **custom collections** — based on theme, rarity, or any other trait.

The same gift can appear in **multiple collections**, letting you create unlimited combinations. Collections also support [granular filters](/blog/wear-gifts-blockchain-and-more/#filter-gifts-on-channel-profiles) for easy browsing.

> To add gifts to a collection, open the _'Gifts'_ tab on your profile or in channel, select a gift and tap _'Add Collection'_.

#### Profile Rating

Telegram profiles now show a **badge** with a **numerical rating** based on the total volume of **successful transactions** they've made with Telegram Stars.

It highlights your Telegram level and **helps channel owners** see that you can be trusted for [Suggested Posts](/blog/checklists-suggested-posts/#suggest-posts-in-channels) and other requests.

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400827/4/Fg9v3tWzU9I.54773.jpg/678d4806dbd265fbd4" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Profile Rating"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400343/2/wLoydHe89uc.1005611.mp4/edbacf4756dae3ae8e" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

> Purchasing gifts, sending [Paid Messages](/blog/star-messages-gateway-2-0-and-more/#stay-in-control-of-your-inbox-with-star-messages) and funding Suggested Posts with Stars **increases your rating**. However, if you **refund Star purchases** or **convert gifts** to Stars your rating will decrease.

#### Premium Gifts

Telegram Gifts are **so popular** that new releases [sell](https://t.me/durov/401) [out](https://t.me/durov/432) in [minutes](https://t.me/durov/433). While you can always buy gifts from others on the [gift marketplace](/blog/gift-marketplace-and-more/#buy-rare-gifts-in-the-marketplace), we want more people to get gifts right at launch.

Soon, you may see a **new type of gift** — available only to Telegram Premium users, with each person able to buy just a **limited number**. Stay tuned!

> The vast majority of Telegram Gifts can still be purchased **by all users** — only **specific sets** of limited-edition gifts will require Premium to purchase.

#### BotFather Mini App

Developers of Telegram [bots and mini apps](/bots/) can now manage them in the new [@BotFather](http://t.me/botfather) **mini app** — from creating bots to updating settings, icons, and descriptions.

Any developer can create sleek mini apps that support [full-screen mode](/blog/fullscreen-miniapps-and-more/), subscriptions, home screen shortcuts, geolocation, motion tracking — and more.
