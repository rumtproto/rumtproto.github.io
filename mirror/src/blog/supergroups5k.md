# Supergroups 5000: Public Groups, Pinned Posts

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140697/2/H2YxbMSil0Y/ff98a308093ebc1282" class="blog_side_image">

Telegram is famous for its awesome [group chats](/faq/). Today’s update makes them even more awesome. To begin with, we’re increasing the number of people who can join a supergroup from 1000 to **5000 members**. That's 5 times as good.

Supergroup admins can now inform all members about important news using pinned messages. **Pinned messages** are displayed at the top of the chat screen. All members will get a notification — even if they muted ordinary messages from the group.

<div class="blog_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/811140258/1/iGyKYxEDslg/29340a1170113b3670" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140258/1/iGyKYxEDslg/29340a1170113b3670" title="A pinned message"></a><p>A pinned message</p></div>

Starting today, supergroups can become **public** and get a short link, like [telegram.me/publictestgroup](https://telegram.me/publictestgroup). This way, anybody can view the group's entire chat history and join to post messages.

<div class="blog_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/811140974/1/4auqDoAJlR8/2ea4146ed3b254bf4a" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140974/1/4auqDoAJlR8/2ea4146ed3b254bf4a" title="A public group"></a><p>A public group</p></div>

We know what you’re thinking. What about spammers? To prevent potential spam issues, we're introducing powerful **moderation tools** for group admins. From now on, they can quickly delete all messages from a specific member, block and report them.

<div class="blog_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/811140789/1/_Jfevhj9PpM/786bd36b900cae8d0d" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140789/1/_Jfevhj9PpM/786bd36b900cae8d0d" title="New admin tools"></a><p>New tools for admins</p></div>

These features work only in **supergroups**, but starting today, any group can be converted to a supergroup by its creator. Group admins are now shown in group members’ lists, so ping your group admin if you think this would be cool.

> Good old groups still work great for smaller communities, so there's no need to convert your group right away.

And that's it for today, watch out for more updates coming soon!

  

_March 13, 2016  
The Telegram Team_

  

**P.S.** Please note that we’ll be rolling out public groups gradually. They’re already live in Europe and America, but are currently not available in several countries in Asia, from which we detected significant spam activity in the past.

We’ll be making the feature available in more countries as we improve our anti-spam algorithms and scale our abuse team.
