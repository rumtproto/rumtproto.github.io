# Telegram Stars: Pay for Digital Goods and More

[<img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400275/2/HQirxDLbG6U.97714.webp/1111a1121d9b762448" srcset="/file/400780400951/5/_5SWZUmwqrk.284290.webp/c3d85b6123302da2c1, 1200w" title="" alt="Telegram Stars: Pay for Digital Goods and More">](https://telegram.org/file/400780400951/5/_5SWZUmwqrk.284290.webp/c3d85b6123302da2c1)

Over **400 million users** interact with **bots** and **mini apps** on Telegram every month – to buy products, access services, play games, and [much more](/bots/). With today's update, bots and mini apps can **sell digital goods and services**.

#### Digital Goods and Services

For years, businesses have been able to **sell physical products** on Telegram via bots and mini apps and [accept payments](/blog/payments-2-0-scheduled-voice-chats/#payments-2-0) from around the world. This month, we updated the [Bot Platform](/bots/features/) to seamlessly support payments for **digital products** as well.

#### Telegram Stars

To enable payments for digital goods and services across the Telegram ecosystem, we're launching **Telegram Stars**. Stars can be acquired through **in-app purchases** via Apple and Google or [PremiumBot](https://t.me/premiumbot), then spent on **digital products** offered by bots – from **e-books** and **online courses** to items in [Telegram games](/bots/#host-games).

> You can try out payments with Stars in [@DurgerKingBot](https://t.me/durgerkingbot) which offers a special _cake_ for the occasion (and even if the cake is a lie, it will refund your stars).

#### Ready for App Stores

Telegram Stars are **compliant** with Apple and Google's latest policies on sales of digital products – and gives developers in the Telegram ecosystem and their users unprecedented access to in-app purchases on mobile app stores.

Soon, developers will be able to withdraw the stars earned by their bots in Toncoins via [Fragment](https://fragment.com/).

> Purchases of **physical products** stay **exactly the same** – using any of Telegram's supported [payment providers](/bots/payments/). Developers can read more about the changes for **digital products** on [this page](/bots/api-changelog/#may-28-2024).

With fast, user-friendly payments for digital goods and services, the bot and mini app platform will allow new types of businesses to reach over **900 million** potential customers inside Telegram.

#### More than Bots

This is **just the beginning** for Telegram Stars. Future updates will bring extra features and functionality to Stars – like **gifts for content creators** and more.
