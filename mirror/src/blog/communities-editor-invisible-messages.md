### Rich Text Editor

We know you can barely express yourself in words. So today, we're giving you more GIFs an **advanced text editor** with support for headings, tables, lists, quotes and code blocks! It also lets you add **photos and videos** in the middle of a message.

Don't worry, the editor comes with an intuitive, **visual interface** — and you can even use our [Cocoon-powered](https://t.me/durov/462) **AI tools** to generate “your” text with full privacy.

### Telegram Communities

Several groups, channels, and bots can now be linked together into **communities** around a shared topic. Community members can **see its other chats** and join them instantly. Starting a cult has never been easier!

<div class="blog_video_player_wrap" style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto 20px;"><div><video class="blog_video_player tl_blog_vid_autoplay" onclick="videoTogglePlay(this)" autoplay="" loop="" controls="" muted="" poster="https://telegram.org/file/400780400627/2/m0_w0ABzq9E.64892.jpg/dd79c8957289c99b38" style="max-width: 600px;" alt="Telegram Communities"><source src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400937/7/7FrlfQ3X9TA.9490984.mp4/905d7975740c1999ae" type="video/mp4"></video></div></div>

Chats in a community can be _Visible_ to **all members** or _Hidden_ from everyone except **community admins** and members of that chat. Cult 101: Always keep a hidden recipe-sharing group for your High Priests.

> Communities **save space** in your chat list. You can toggle whether a community appears as **separate chats** or as **one chat** that expands when you open it.

Communities are **collaborative** by default, allowing any member to **add more chats** — but you can **restrict** this in settings before your cultists add 47 cat groups. When adding chats is limited, you will see them as **suggestions** instead.

### Ephemeral Bot Messages

With [guest bot mode](/blog/ai-bot-revolution-11-new-features/#guest-bots), you could already get information from bots **in any chat**. After today’s update, these messages will no longer clutter the group chat for everyone else.

Bots in groups can now send messages visible only to a **specific person**, such as **greeting messages**, **private AI summaries**, errors, confirmations, or menus with buttons.

<div class="blog_image_wrap blog_wide_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/400780400146/2/NCkBKEh9JJ0.148639.jpg/abe830e0723e38a4f9" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/400780400146/2/NCkBKEh9JJ0.148639.jpg/abe830e0723e38a4f9" alt="Ephemeral Messages" srcset="/file/400780400146/2/NCkBKEh9JJ0.148639.jpg/abe830e0723e38a4f9 , 2x"></a></div>

Bots can send ephemeral messages at any time or in response to **specific commands**. These commands are highlighted in the bot menu with a **special icon**, and the resulting messages are visible only to the bot and the person who used them.

> Bot developers can learn more about ephemeral messages [here](/bots/features/#ephemeral-messages).

### 350 Million GIFs

Did we just say we were not giving you more GIFs? We lied. The GIF panel now lets you search over **350 million GIFs** in **36 languages**. The new search is powered by **offline indexing** of widely shared, publicly available GIFs with [open-source AI models](https://cocoon.org/).

Unlike in all other messaging apps, your Telegram GIF searches are **entirely private**, **don't rely** on any third parties — and are now **much faster**.
