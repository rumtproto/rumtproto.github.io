# Edit Messages, New Mentions and More

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140010/1/POGV5NPdHAo.23437.gif/f1389ee360bf61f577" class="blog_side_image">

Farewell to typos! Starting today, you can edit the text of your messages after sending them. This works across all Telegram chats, including groups and one-on-one conversations.

<div class="blog_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/811140100/2/maZcBXgwrmE.306486/5bd7c8f4708afe28f8" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140100/2/maZcBXgwrmE.306486/5bd7c8f4708afe28f8" title="Edit messages"></a><p>Edit messages</p></div>

Simply tap and hold on a message, then press ‘Edit’. If you're on [desktop](https://desktop.telegram.org), press the up arrow button to edit your last message. The messages will display a small ‘edited’ label so that it's easy to tell which were altered.

#### New Mentions

[Mentioning](/blog/replies-mentions-hashtags/) other people in groups is handy since it sends them a notification about your message even if they muted the group. Starting today, you can mention any members in a group – even if they don't have a [username](/blog/usernames-and-secret-chats-v2/). Just type the `@` symbol and select whoever you would like to address. Easy!

<div class="blog_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/811140709/1/gTAMe7KBjxI.213591/37b9d9f3090d78a47f" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140709/1/gTAMe7KBjxI.213591/37b9d9f3090d78a47f" title="Mention group members"></a><p>Mention group members</p></div>

#### People List

Speaking of addressing people, you can now get to your recent chats much faster using the new People list in Search.

<div class="blog_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/811140588/2/TcLgRm4lvOs.216480/400f4e745ca4c23d05" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140588/2/TcLgRm4lvOs.216480/400f4e745ca4c23d05" title="People"></a><p>Recent chats</p></div>

#### Bot Attachments

We've also made it easier for you to access your favorite [inline bots](/blog/inline-bots/). Simply scroll down the attachment menu – and there they are. The more you use them, the higher they will climb.

<div class="blog_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/811140151/2/QBr-eVQIcDQ.234803/03ff1e9b0848871394" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140151/2/QBr-eVQIcDQ.234803/03ff1e9b0848871394" title="Bots in attachment menu"></a><p>Bots in attachment menu</p></div>

Naturally, you will only see inline bots in the attachment menu if you used them at least once. Try [@youtube](https://telegram.me/youtube), [@gif](https://telegram.me/gif) or [@imdb](https://telegram.me/imdb) if you don't know where to start. Check out [this post](/blog/inline-bots/) for more info on how to use inline bots.

#### Interface Improvements

We‘ve added quick sharing buttons to forwarded messages from bots, channels, and public groups. Notifications about messages with stickers will now show the relevant emoji so that you’ll know the general idea at first glance.

<div class="blog_image_wrap"><a href="https://telegram.org/file/811140465/2/t_gAyjk4S3A.61521/73620590fdc021aa4e" target="_blank"><img src="https://telegram.org/file/811140465/2/t_gAyjk4S3A.61521/73620590fdc021aa4e" title="Scroll to bottom"></a><p>Scroll to bottom</p></div>

Last but not least, if you're on [iOS](https://telegram.org/dl/ios), your app now remembers the scroll position in chats when switching to a different chat and back. And scrolling up in a chat summons a new button that will send you back to the bottom in one tap. This button also displays a handy unread message counter if new ones are waiting for you there.

  

And that's it for today. Stay tuned for more updates coming soon!
