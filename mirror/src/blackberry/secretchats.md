# Secret Chats

Messages in Secret Chats can't be forwarded to other users and have a self-destruct timer. Secret Chats are identified by a lock icon next to them.

#### 1\. Empty Chat

  

<img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140785/2/USeao40Fiug/8d41715c0b6bde8530" width="360" height="360">

  

#### 2\. Secret Chat Info

Opened by tapping on the header. The info page contains self-desctruct timer settings and encryption key visualization. The pencil icon allows [editing the contact](/blackberry/contacts/).

  

<img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140972/1/PAgJ17eimGc/f698c5c5df6dc2ecc4" width="360" height="360">

  

#### 3\. Self-Destruct Timer

Users can set a self-desruct interval ranging from 1 second to 1 week. The options are: off || 1s || 2s || 3s || 4s || 5s || 6s || 7s || 8s || 9s || 10s || 11s || 12s || 13s || 14s || 15s || 1m || 1h || 1d || 1w.

  

<img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140800/1/FvpBs20xfIU/0c9fff5e503dfb91e7" width="360" height="360">

  

#### 4\. Encryption Key Visualization

  

<img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140285/1/UHRP68uffV4/9746b22c32e18ae1ea" width="360" height="360">

  

#### 5\. Self-Destructing Images

On shorter timers of **1 minute and less** image previews are not shown in the chat. They are instead heavily blurred, have a flame icon and can only be viewed while the user keeps his finger pressed on the photo.

  

<img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140534/2/mmLEcD0iuCg/1bc3d30c5f62c447a2" width="360" height="360">

  

#### 6\. Countdown

As soon as the user views a photo, the self-destruct timer goes off. Countdown can be seen both in the photo viewer and in the chat list.

  

<img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140324/2/nHAC2PQu8sA/1d7297873a25e3a7db" width="360" height="360"> <img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140484/3/WeSPT-1r7tg/cc228faf61fd663d7c" width="360" height="360">

  

#### 7\. Outgoing Self-Destructing Images

On the sender's side no countdown is show. Once the timer starts, the flame icon is substituted by a big checkmark. The photo is then deleted as soon as the time runs out.

  

<img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140257/1/PfMpB2J5zZM/a21e1f6982e5d8fe1e" width="360" height="360">
