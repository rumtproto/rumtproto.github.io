# Viewing Media

Telegram supports four kinds of attachments: photos, videos, documents and locations. Photos, videos and documents need to be downloaded before they can be viewed. Photos are downloaded automatically once the user starts viewing the chat, and the downloading of videos and documents is initiated explicitly by a tap on the content (this behaviour can be changed by the user in the Settings section).

#### 1\. Downloading Media

A radial progress indicator is shown over downloading items. The download process may be paused by tapping the pause icon inside the radial progress indicator.

#### 2\. Media in Group Chats

Incoming media in group chats is displayed inside the message bubbles next to the name and the profile picture of the sender.

  

<img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140592/1/mD-WBPkJEDk/4e024b09e961f8abde" width="360" height="360"> <img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140038/1/XSYS7kibQc4/e8cdbdb977f9856962" width="360" height="360">

  

#### 3\. Viewing Photos And Videos

Photos and videos are opened in a media viewer. The user can swipe left to navigate through all media shared in the respective chat. Zoom and pan are supported. The action menu allows forwarding media to a different chat, saving it to the gallery or deleting. Users can also access the **Shared Media** screen from here.

  

<img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140182/1/M29WlQMRvM0/7e17c04b1d1eb4a376" width="360" height="360"> <img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140245/1/h3qC0eiFuZ0/662ddac6fb80eaab0a" width="360" height="360">

  

#### 4\. Shared media

The title of this screen is **Shared media** in case of individual users and **Media in group** for groups.

  

<img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140766/1/rq-YZc99ZDc/0b8243b8b73af3c670" width="360" height="360">

  

#### 5\. Viewing Locations

The Map View displays the coordinate shared by the sender, the current location of the viewing user and the distance between the two points on the map.

  

<img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140532/1/mtUyVWTJB5U/7529fba582cc29c001" width="360" height="360"> <img src="https://core.telegram.org/file/811140448/1/oPw-YdgkPUs/ddaaa00d46d73cf571" width="360" height="360">

  

#### 6\. GIF playback

Downloaded gifs display a **play** and are played inline when tapped. You can tap them again to stop playback.
