## A (Not So) Brief History of Telegram

This story is written backwards – from the latest update to the primordial summer of **2013**. So if you're a fan of chronology, you can [jump to the end](#august-14-2013) and scroll up from there. This page gets **updated** each time a new post hits the [Telegram blog](/blog/).
