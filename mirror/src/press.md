# Telegram Press Info

<img src="https://telegram.org/file/464001909/10e80/L-CdrdgBypo.36352.png/a5d1b618cb9e8740c4">

**Telegram Messenger** is one of the [top-5 most downloaded apps](https://www.forbes.com/sites/johnkoetsier/2021/12/27/top-10-most-downloaded-apps-and-games-of-2021-tiktok-telegram-big-winners/) in the world. In 2025, it passed **1 billion** monthly active users. The company is based in **Dubai**.

Telegram is committed to protecting user privacy and human rights such as freedom of speech and assembly. It has played a prominent role in pro-democracy movements [around the world](https://www.theguardian.com/media/2020/nov/07/nobody-can-block-it-how-telegram-app-fuels-global-protest), including in [Iran](https://www.wsj.com/articles/iranians-turn-to-telegram-app-amid-protests-1514919114), [Russia](https://www.washingtonpost.com/opinions/2020/06/23/how-tech-company-prevailed-against-state-putins-russia/), [Belarus](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2021/05/28/belarus-forced-down-plane-because-it-couldnt-shut-down-an-app/), [Myanmar](https://www.dw.com/en/the-battle-for-myanmar-plays-out-on-twitter-tiktok-and-telegram/a-57267075) and [Hong Kong](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/hong-kong-s-student-protesters-catch-class-same-way-they-n1082776).

**Pavel Durov** is the founder, owner and CEO of Telegram. He left Russia in 2014, after losing control of his previous company for refusing to hand over the data of [Ukrainian protesters](https://www.nytimes.com/2014/12/03/technology/once-celebrated-in-russia-programmer-pavel-durov-chooses-exile.html) to security agencies. Pavel Durov lives in Dubai and holds a dual citizenship of the United Arab Emirates and France.

#### Security

Everything sent on Telegram is **securely encrypted**. Researchers can independently verify the app's security using Telegram's [open source code](/apps/#source-code), fully-documented [encryption protocols](/mtproto/) and [reproducible builds](/reproducible-builds/).

Telegram has an ongoing [bug bounty program](/bug-bounty/) that rewards those who find any potential flaws in our security so they can be fixed. To date, no way to break the Telegram encryption has ever been found.

[More about security in the Telegram FAQ »](/faq/#security)

#### Privacy

Unlike other messaging and social media apps, Telegram collects **no data** about its users – not even a name or a birthday – only a [phone number](/privacy/#3-1-basic-account-data) is needed to sign up. Telegram’s [sustainable monetization model](https://t.me/durov/142) allows the app to cover its development costs while [protecting the privacy](https://t.me/durov/176) of its users.

[More in Telegram's Privacy Policy »](/privacy/)

#### Verified information

We believe the most effective way to [combat misinformation](https://t.me/durov/163) is to give readers the tools and information to scrutinize the media they consume.

Telegram is used by [news outlets](https://www.nytimes.com/2022/04/06/insider/following-our-audience-to-telegram.html), [fact-checking organizations](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/media/entertainment/media/ib-ministry-launches-account-on-telegram-for-fact-checking/articleshow/86193616.cms) and [health ministries](https://t.me/corona/22) to broadcast **verified information** directly to millions of users.
